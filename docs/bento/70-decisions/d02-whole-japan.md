# D2: Data scope: the whole of Japan, in depth

## Context
A Japan planner that only knows a few cities is a city guide.

## Decision
The target is every destination in Japan at full depth, roughly 2,000 place records.

## Consequences
It forces tiered coverage (see [coverage](../10-product/coverage.md)) and a routing engine for transit.

## Today
The target stands. Today Bento plans Kyoto, Osaka, Nara, Hiroshima, Miyajima and Himeji (the Kansai–Sanyo corridor) and Tokyo, and says plainly when it doesn't know a place well enough.
