# D15: First deep cities: the Kansai–Sanyo corridor

## Context
Deep coverage needs a connected train network, and curating everywhere at once isn't possible.

## Decision
Osaka, Kyoto, Nara and Hiroshima first, about 160 records, joined by one train network.

## Consequences
One contiguous network covers all four cities.

## Today
Done, and extended: Miyajima and Himeji (an en-route stop) were added, and later Tokyo with its own network (see [coverage](../10-product/coverage.md)).
