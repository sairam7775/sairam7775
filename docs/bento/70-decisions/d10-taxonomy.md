# D10: Taxonomy: about 10 tags and 3 style axes

## Context
A flat list of interests can't say "nature, but not hiking".

## Decision
About ten interest tags plus three style axes (such as energy and crowd tolerance).

## Consequences
Tags drive interest matching; the axes drive the penalties in scoring (see [engine](../30-software/engine.md)).

## Today
In effect (`user_preferences`, migration 0002).
