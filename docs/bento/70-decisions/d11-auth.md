# D11: Sign-in: Google, and email with password

## Context
Travellers expect both; sign-in had to be real from the start because it protects booking references.

## Decision
Google sign-in, and email with password, with password reset and sign-in rate limits.

## Consequences
Password reset and rate limiting were needed from the first phase.

## Today
In effect. Google sign-in went live on 12 November 2026. Sign-in limits per visitor, email and account were added in November 2026 (see [security](../40-non-functional/security.md)).
