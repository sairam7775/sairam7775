# Bento Man, animated — design

Date: 2026-10-19 · Status: approved look, spec awaiting the owner's review

## Why

The owner asked for Bento Man animations "so that users feel connected to the character". Today Bento Man is only the Bento logo: a box with four compartments and an umeboshi, shown beside his chat replies and as the hopping-umeboshi loader. He has no body, face or moods.

Success means a traveller meets a recognisable little guide who reacts to what happens (he waves when you arrive, thinks while he works, cheers when you accept a change, looks lost on a missing page), without the app getting slower, harder to use, or less accessible.

## Decisions the owner made

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| Character | **C · The guide**: a small local guide with a bento-box head |
| Head | The first C layout as a **lacquer box**: dark rim with a subtle highlight, four white compartments, the umeboshi top-left, two eyes in the lower compartments. No food, no cheeks, no mouth |
| Body | Follows the Canva concept (reference only, not shipped): **indigo happi coat** with wide sleeves and a white collar band, a tied dark-blue belt, peach mitten hands, grey trousers, white socks, sandals, and a folded **map** he always carries |
| Where | Chat, trips page, home page, the tour, the 404 and error pages |
| How it's built | **Lottie** animations, generated by Claude from the drawing (no animator) |
| Concept art | Canva AI images were inspiration only. The shipped character is original artwork drawn for Bento, so it stays ownable (the JPO trademark is on the board) |

The approved drawings are in [`2026-10-19-bento-man/`](2026-10-19-bento-man/): one animated SVG per mood, light and dark, plus `comparison.png` (the Canva concept beside the final guide). They are the visual reference for the build; the coordinates in them are the starting point for the art data below.

## The character

**Anatomy** (one 96 × 132 drawing; the head alone is 64 × 64):

- **Head**: rounded lacquer box (rim, highlight line, four white compartments with soft corners), umeboshi at the top-left compartment's centre, eyes in the lower two compartments.
- **Body**: happi coat with collar band and tied belt; sleeves are thick outlined strokes from the shoulders, ending in mitten hands; trousers, socks and sandals below.
- **Prop**: a three-panel folded map with a dotted salmon route.

**Colours.** Every part belongs to a colour role, and each role is a CSS variable, so the figure follows the theme:

| Role | Light | Dark | Parts |
|---|---|---|---|
| `line` | ink #221C1E | rice #FBF8F3 | outlines, legs, sandal edges |
| `rim` / `sheen` | #221C1E / #5A5256 | #4A4145 / #7A7075 | the lacquer box |
| `tile` | #FFFFFF | #FFFFFF | compartments (the head looks the same in both themes) |
| `face` | #221C1E | #221C1E | eyes and eye shapes (drawn on white tiles) |
| `coat` / `belt` | #2E4A7D / #1C2E52 | #5876B5 / #2E4A7D | coat, sleeves, belt |
| fixed | — | — | umeboshi #FF7757, collar and socks white, hands #F6CFB3, map paper #F4E9D6, sparkles #FFC94A, muted marks ink-3 |

**Forms.** *Full* (the whole guide) for big moments; *head* (the box alone) where space is tight (24–40 px). Eyes are large enough to read at 24 px.

## The five moods

| Mood | Full figure | Head | Plays |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Resting** | sways gently, blinks; map hangs at his side | blinks; umeboshi bobs | idle loop every mood settles into |
| **Thinking** | hand on chin, reads the map, eyes up, umeboshi hops between the top compartments, thought dots | eyes up, umeboshi hops | loops while Bento Man works |
| **Happy** | jumps with both arms up, waves the map, eyes ^ ^, umeboshi pops, sparkles | eyes ^ ^, umeboshi pops, sparkles | once, then resting |
| **Hello** | waves, winks, map in the other hand | winks, umeboshi bobs up | once, then resting |
| **Lost** | scratches his head, holds the map up, head tilts as his eyes search left and right, a "?" rises | — | once, then resting |

Each animation's **first frame is that mood's still pose**, so swapping the still drawing for the animation never jumps.

## Files and how they are made

- **Art data** — `bento/src/components/mascot/art.ts`: every shape, colour role and pivot point, and each mood's pose and keyframes, as plain data. The one source of truth.
- **Still drawing** — `bento/src/components/mascot/still.tsx`: renders a mood's still pose as inline SVG from the art data (server-rendered, no JavaScript needed).
- **Generator** — `bento/scripts/build-mascot.ts`, run with `npx tsx scripts/build-mascot.ts`: turns the art data into Lottie JSON in `bento/public/mascot/`. Each shape layer carries its colour role as a class name (`cl: "bm-coat"` and so on).
- **Files shipped** (only what a screen uses): `full-hello.json`, `full-lost.json`, `head-think.json`, `head-happy.json`, `head-hello.json`. Each has an `intro` and an `idle` marker. Target: under 25 KB each before compression. The full-figure thinking and happy moods are drawn in the art data but not generated until a screen needs them.
- **Staleness test** — a Vitest test runs the generator in memory and fails if a committed file differs, so the drawing and the files can't drift apart.

## Playing him on a page

One component, `<BentoManFigure mood form size />`:

1. The server renders the still pose in a box of fixed size. No layout shift, works without JavaScript.
2. In the browser, once the figure scrolls into view and the traveller hasn't asked for reduced motion, it loads the Lottie light player (`lottie-web/build/player/lottie_light`, 46 KB compressed, measured; loaded once and shared) and the mood's file, then swaps the still for the animation.
3. It plays the `intro` segment once, then rests still on the first idle frame (the owner's decision after review, 2026-10-19: WCAG 2.2.2, no self-started motion past five seconds). Only the thinking head, which has no intro, loops, and only while Bento Man works. It pauses when scrolled off-screen or when the tab is hidden.
4. **Reduced motion**: the still pose only. The player is never downloaded.
5. **Failure**: if the player or a file fails to load, the still pose stays. Nothing else changes.
6. **Colours**: `globals.css` maps the layer classes (`.bm-line`, `.bm-coat`, …) to `--color-mascot-*` variables, with dark values in the existing dark-theme blocks. lottie-web's SVG renderer writes colours as attributes, which CSS overrides.
7. **Accessibility**: the figure is decoration (`aria-hidden`). Every message it accompanies stays as text. Nothing flashes.
8. **Every visit**: *hello* plays each time its page loads, then settles into resting. (Owner's decision at the plan stage, 2026-10-19: remembering a visit would need device storage the cookie policy would then have to list; a 2–3 second wave per visit is the simpler choice.)

No new Content-Security-Policy is needed (the app sets only `frame-ancestors`, and the light player doesn't use `eval`).

## Where he appears

| Place | Form, size | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| Chat header | head, 28 px | still resting (replaces the logo mark) |
| Beside each reply | head, 24 px | still |
| A proposal just accepted | head, 32 px | **happy** once, where the card's buttons were, beside "It's in your plan." (changed during the build: the reply's avatar is often scrolled out of view by then) |
| "Working…" line | head, 20 px | **thinking** while Bento Man works, replacing the mini loader; the visible label and its live region stay |
| Empty chat | full, ~104 px | **hello** beside the intro and example prompts |
| Trips page | full, ~72 px | **hello** beside はじめまして / おかえりなさい |
| Home page | full, ~64 px | **hello** beside the sample day's Bento Man note, replacing its small logo (the preview stays hidden from screen readers) |
| Tour | head, 36 px | **hello** on the first card, still on the rest |
| 404 and error pages | full, ~120 px | **lost** above the message (`StatusPage` gains an optional figure). `global-error` is unchanged: it renders without the app's styles |

**Unchanged:** the logo in headers, the favicon and app icons, and the page loader (`BentoLoader`): the plain logo stays the brand mark.

## Checks

- **Unit tests**: generator staleness; Lottie sanity (version, frame rate, size, markers, every layer's class is a known role); play rules (the proposed → accepted trigger, the reduced-motion gate, which figures have a file, the intro/idle segments).
- **Browser**, at 390 px and desktop, light and dark: each placement plays and settles; reduced motion shows stills and downloads no player; a blocked player leaves the stills.
- **Accessibility**: axe (WCAG 2.2 AA) on chat, trips, home and 404, light and dark: no new findings.
- **Performance**: Lighthouse on home and trips before and after. Layout shift stays 0; Largest Contentful Paint no worse than 100 ms; the player is absent from the first-load bundle.
- **Codex review** on the PR, as usual.

## Out of scope

Interactivity (eyes following the pointer, reacting to taps), sound, a Rive version, changes to the plain logo or loader, and any change to what Bento Man says (so no model eval is needed).

## Risks

- **Hand-built Lottie quality.** Generating Lottie from code is unusual. *Mitigation:* the build plan starts with a spike: one file (`head-think`) in one place, checked in the browser against the still, before the rest.
- **Theming through layer classes.** *Mitigation:* the spike also proves that the CSS variables recolour a Lottie layer in dark mode; if not, the generator writes one file per theme instead.
- **Bundle and speed.** *Mitigation:* lazy player, fixed boxes, the Lighthouse comparison above.
