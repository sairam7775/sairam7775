Nine nights, four cities

You asked for six cities. I’ve cut it to four, and here’s the arithmetic. Adding Tokyo and Kanazawa costs 9h40m on trains — more than a full waking day of your nine, spent moving. You’d see six places badly instead of four properly.

Tokyo — 2h15 each way from Kyoto Kanazawa — 2h20, and I only have it at outline

Say the word and I’ll put either back — it’s your trip, not my arithmetic.

Osaka 大阪 deep 2 nights

Land here — Kansai airport is 50 minutes away, against 75 to Kyoto. Osaka Castle is the one castle you’ll see without a detour, and the food is the reason people stay an extra night.

JR Special Rapid · 29 min · ¥580 · IC card, no reservation
Kyoto 京都 deep 4 nights

Four nights because shrines and temples is your first interest and Kyoto has more of them than the rest of this route combined. Late November is peak autumn colour — which is also why I’m starting you at 7am most days.

Kintetsu Limited Express · 35 min · ¥1,160 · day trip, bags stay in Kyoto
Nara 奈良 deep day trip

Not worth a night. Todai-ji and the park are one very good day, and sleeping in Kyoto saves you moving luggage twice.

Sanyo Shinkansen · 1h25 · ¥11,200 · stop at Himeji on the way (+3h)
Hiroshima 広島 deep 3 nights

Three because Miyajima needs a whole day and deserves it — Itsukushima at high tide is the one shrine on this trip you’ll still be thinking about in a year. Fly home from Hiroshima rather than backtracking.
